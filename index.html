The wheel

Eight modes at launch

Each mode changes one thing. You don't get to pick which one you play, so it pays to know them all. Click a mode to read the briefing.

Movement

Fast. Really fast.

You're not stuck walking between gunfights. Slide under fire, vault over cover and climb onto anything at chest height without breaking your sprint. The best players barely stop moving.

Slide

Crouch while sprinting to drop into a slide. Get under a peek, cross an open street or catch someone off guard around a corner.

Vault

Hop over fences, car hoods, counters and crates without slowing down. Low cover won't trap you.

Mantle

Grab a ledge and pull yourself up. Windows, rooftops and walls are all routes if you can reach them.

Chain it

Slide into a vault into a mantle. Moves carry your momentum, so a clean chain gets you somewhere before the other team expects you.

Gunplay

The guns feel right

Full body, no floating arms

Look down and you'll see your legs. The animations you see in first person are the same ones other players see.

Proper weapon handling

Reloads, draws and mag checks are all animated. In Blackout, a mag check is the only way to know what you've got left.

Same guns in every mode

The modes mess with the rules, not the weapons. Recoil and handling stay the same, so the practice you put in carries over.
